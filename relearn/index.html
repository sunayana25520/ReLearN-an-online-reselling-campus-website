How to Use ReLearN

1. Sign Up

Create an account using your college email ID. Use your registration number as the default password.

2. Sell Items

List any item by adding its details, condition, and a reasonable price. Upload a picture to make it more attractive.

3. Buy Items

Browse through listings and send a message request to the seller. Decide on a convenient location inside the college to meet.

4. Rate the Seller

After your transaction, you can give a rating to the seller based on your experience. This helps other students.
